Dual-role disclosure script (required when Role = Both / co-serving)

Read or hand this script to the participant / decision-maker. Mark N/A if single role.

What the wellness coach does: Supports your self-chosen wellness goals, habits, and resources. Coaching notes focus on goals, progress, and coaching activities — not clinical diagnosis or treatment.

What the case manager does: Helps with care coordination, program / benefits navigation, and any required care-plan or assessment work for your program. CM notes focus on coordination, eligibility, and plan requirements.

Who may see which notes: Your coach and your case manager (when both are serving you) may each document in their role. Supervision and program staff may review notes as needed under organization policy. Outside parties do not get your information until a completed ROI (or law) allows it.

When role = Both / co-serving: The same person may act as both coach and case manager for you. Both sets of duties and note types can apply. You can ask which hat they are wearing in a given conversation.

Permission to coordinate = intent only. No third-party sharing of PHI/PII until an ROI (or state/payer equivalent) is complete, except as required by law. Complete Section 7 ROI fields (purpose, parties, redisclosure limits, expiration, revoke rights) — or attach official ROI — before any PHI/PII share. For WA sensitive categories, also complete addenda/WA/WA-sensitive-ROI-checklist.md.

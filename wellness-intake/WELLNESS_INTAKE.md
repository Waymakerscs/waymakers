**v1.2 — Policy structural sign-off; pending counsel + program-type map — not approved for live use. Not legal advice.**

**Audience:** wellness coaches and case managers; adult participants by default.

Use this form at intake and when goals change. Complete with the participant. Mark required fields with *. Attach any state-specific addenda required for the service location.

**Org baseline vs state addenda:** Sections 5–6 are the organization baseline (including `addenda/ORG-baseline-rights.md`). State-specific notices and official forms live in the launch addenda packs only (`addenda/OK/`, `addenda/WA/`) and are tracked in Section 7 — not hardcoded multi-state legal text in this baseline. Launch pack must match service delivery state (OK or WA only). Exact program types (which rows are required vs N/A) are TBD from Alexa and will flip status expectations once confirmed.

### Dual-role disclosure script (required when Role = Both / co-serving)
*Read or hand this short script to the participant (or decision-maker). Mark N/A and skip if staff role is a single role.*

> **What the wellness coach does:** Supports your self-chosen wellness goals, habits, and resources. Coaching notes focus on goals, progress, and coaching activities — not clinical diagnosis or treatment.
>
> **What the case manager does:** Helps with care coordination, program / benefits navigation, and any required care-plan or assessment work for your program. CM notes focus on coordination, eligibility, and plan requirements.
>
> **Who may see which notes:** Your coach and your case manager (when both are serving you) may each document in their role. Supervision and program staff may review notes as needed under organization policy. Outside parties do **not** get your information until a completed ROI (or law) allows it.
>
> **When role = Both / co-serving:** The same person may act as both coach and case manager for you. Both sets of duties and note types can apply. You can ask which hat they are wearing in a given conversation.

Participant / decision-maker initials that dual-role script was explained: ______ Date: ______ ☐ N/A (single role)

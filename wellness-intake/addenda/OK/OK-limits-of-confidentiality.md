## What we keep private
Wellness coaching and case management information is kept private under our organization policies and applicable law. We share information only with your permission (via a completed Release of Information / ROI), or when the law or our safety duties require it.

## When we may have to share without your usual permission
Staff may be required to report or disclose information in situations such as:

1. **Imminent risk of harm** — if there is serious and immediate risk that you or someone else may be harmed.
2. **Suspected abuse or neglect of a child** — Oklahoma mandated reporting for children. Staff follow our organization SOP for how and where to report.
3. **Suspected abuse, neglect, or exploitation of a vulnerable adult** — Oklahoma mandated reporting for vulnerable adults. Staff follow our organization SOP.
4. **Court orders or other lawful process** — when a valid court order or similar legal requirement applies.
5. **Other disclosures required by Oklahoma or federal law** for this program.

## What this service is (and is not)
This service supports wellness goals and resources. It does **not** diagnose, treat, or replace medical, mental health, or crisis care. In an emergency, call **911** or your local emergency number.

## Your written rights (summary)
- You may ask how to revoke consent or care-coordination permission; some records and legally required reports may still continue after revoke.
- You may ask how to access or request amendment of your records (organization process).
- You may use the grievance / complaint process explained at intake (see also `ORG-baseline-rights.md`).
- No third-party sharing of your health or personal information until an ROI (or state/payer equivalent) is complete, except as required by law.

## Program notes (Oklahoma)
- If you are in an **ADvantage / HCBS waiver** case-management program, staff use official OK tools and forms (they do not replace them with this intake alone). Wellness-coach-only services mark those items N/A.
- Telehealth / remote rules, advance directives, linguistic access, and grievance timing depend on your specific OK program — staff confirm what applies at intake.

## How this meets the intake “written privacy / rights summary”
For **OK or WA launch**, Section 6 of the Wellness Intake Form treats the written privacy/rights summary as satisfied when you receive **both**:

1. The **state limits-of-confidentiality one-pager** for your launch pack (`addenda/OK/OK-limits-of-confidentiality.md` or `addenda/WA/WA-limits-of-confidentiality.md`), **and**

Receipt initials on the state one-pager are **required** when that launch pack is selected (they confirm you received the written limits). Keep copies with the intake.

## Your rights (plain language)
- You may ask questions before you agree to participate in wellness coaching and/or case management.
- You may refuse or withdraw from services without retaliation. Some legally required record-keeping or safety reports may still continue after you revoke consent or coordination permission.
- You may ask how to revoke consent or care-coordination permission, and what continues after revoke.
- You may ask how to access or request amendment of your records using the organization process explained at intake.
- Your information is kept private under organization policy and applicable law. Outside parties do not get your health or personal information until a completed Release of Information (ROI) — or the law — allows it. “Permission to coordinate” on the intake is **intent only**, not a share authorization.
- Records are kept for the period set in the organization retention schedule (staff will state the period that applies to your program).

## Grievance / complaint (plain language)
If you have a concern about how you were treated, about privacy, or about services:

1. Tell your coach or case manager, **or**
2. Ask for the organization grievance / complaint contact explained at intake (Privacy Officer / Client Rights / Quality — per org SOP), **and/or**
3. If you have Medicaid / MCO / state-program coverage, you may also use member rights and appeals processes in your handbook (staff attach those pages when required).

Staff will not retaliate because you raised a concern.

## What this service is (and is not)
This service supports wellness goals and resources. It does **not** diagnose, treat, or replace medical, mental health, or crisis care. In an emergency, call **911** or your local emergency number. Staff also follow the organization crisis protocol.

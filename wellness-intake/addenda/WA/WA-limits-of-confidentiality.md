## What we keep private
Wellness coaching and case management information is kept private under our organization policies and applicable law. Washington may have health-care confidentiality rules that go beyond federal HIPAA for some services. Whether those rules apply depends on the program and whether this service is treated as a health care provider under Washington law for your program — staff verify with organization counsel / compliance (they do not guess on this form).

We share information only with your permission (via a completed Release of Information / ROI), or when the law or our safety duties require it.

## When we may have to share without your usual permission
Staff may be required to report or disclose information in situations such as:

1. **Imminent risk of harm** — if there is serious and immediate risk that you or someone else may be harmed.
2. **Suspected abuse or neglect of a child** — Washington mandated reporting. Staff follow our organization SOP.
3. **Suspected abuse, neglect, or exploitation of a vulnerable adult (APS)** — Washington adult protective reporting. Staff follow our organization SOP.
4. **Court orders or other lawful process** — when a valid court order or similar legal requirement applies.
5. **Other disclosures required by Washington or federal law** for this program.

## Sensitive information (extra protection)
Some information — including mental health, HIV/STD, and substance use (including 42 CFR Part 2 when applicable) — may need **separate, clear permission** on an ROI before sharing. If we ask to share those categories, staff will use the WA sensitive-ROI checklist and program / HCA forms, not a general “permission to coordinate” check alone.

## What this service is (and is not)
This service supports wellness goals and resources. It does **not** diagnose, treat, or replace medical, mental health, or crisis care. In an emergency, call **911** or your local emergency number.

## Your written rights (summary)
- You may ask how to revoke consent or care-coordination permission; some records and legally required reports may still continue after revoke.
- You may ask how to access or request amendment of your records (organization process).
- You may use the grievance / complaint process explained at intake (see also `ORG-baseline-rights.md`).
- If you have **Apple Health / MCO / BH-ASO** coverage, member rights and appeals information from your handbook may also apply — staff attach those pages when required.
- No third-party sharing of your health or personal information until an ROI (or state/payer equivalent) is complete, except as required by law.

## Program notes (Washington)
- Telemedicine (including audio-only when applicable) follows current HCA / Apple Health rules when those programs apply — staff verify at intake.
- Exact program type for your case may change which items are required vs N/A.
